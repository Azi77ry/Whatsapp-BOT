⚡ About KnightBot Multi-Session Engine

KnightBot-MD is a state-of-the-art multi-tenant WhatsApp bot framework powered by Baileys. It allows connecting and managing 50+ independent concurrent WhatsApp sessions simultaneously with isolated encrypted credential stores, real-time telemetry, and automated anti-ban protections.

📱 Method 1: Pairing Code Linking

Enter your international phone number in the dashboard to receive an instant 8-digit verification code. Link directly via WhatsApp > Linked Devices > Link with phone number.

📷 Method 2: QR Code Scanning

Generate a high-resolution dynamic QR code on the dashboard and scan it in seconds with your phone camera.

🛡️ Privacy & Zero Logging

Direct Noise-protocol socket encryption. Chat logs, media, and private contacts are never stored or forwarded to third-party servers.

🔌 1-Click Instant Disconnect

Revoke bot access anytime directly from the Web Dashboard or in the WhatsApp app (*Linked Devices > Log Out*).
