Terms of Service & Data Security Protocol

End-to-End Encrypted & Private

Knight Bot runs direct WhatsApp Baileys protocol with strict zero-knowledge chat isolation.

1. Data Privacy & Storage

We never log, read, store, or sell your private conversations, media, or contact lists. Auth tokens are stored in an isolated, encrypted local sandbox.

2. What the Bot Does

The bot only responds when triggered by explicit commands (e.g. .menu, .sticker, .ai, .song, .tagall). It does not send random or unprompted messages.

3. Terms of Service

You agree to use the bot responsibly and ethically. Using the bot for malicious spamming, bulk harassment, or unlawful content is strictly prohibited.

4. Instant Revocation

You can disconnect the bot at any second by clicking Disconnect on this dashboard or directly in your WhatsApp phone app via Linked Devices > Log Out.

Your Safety Guarantees

  • Official WhatsApp Web Protocol: Connects using WhatsApp's multi-device web socket engine.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: No telemetry is forwarded to third-party ad networks or external tracking databases.
  • Full User Control: You can pause the bot or edit its configuration at any time.
